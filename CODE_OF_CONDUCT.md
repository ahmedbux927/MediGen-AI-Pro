# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in this project's spaces — issues, discussions and
reviews — is expected to be respectful and constructive.

- Be kind and professional; critique ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Keep discussion on-topic and helpful for other pharmacy users and
  developers.

The project owner may remove comments or block accounts that break these
rules. Report problems privately via the contact details in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
